About the demo

A bookstore made for curious readers—and Adobe Target learners.

The Reading Nook is a sample digital bookstore. Its titles, descriptions, and merchandising messages are illustrative, so teams can practice experimentation without changing a real customer experience.

The homepage contains three clearly marked test locations: a headline, a supporting message, and a call-to-action. They are built with simple, stable HTML so Adobe Target's Visual Experience Composer can edit them independently.

What you can learn here

Practice authoring and QA for A/B and Multivariate Tests, compare different content combinations, and connect Web SDK events to reporting when your Adobe configuration is ready.
